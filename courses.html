How do I access the course after purchasing?
Immediately after successful enrollment, you will receive your personal login credentials via email and WhatsApp. You can watch the video lessons from your phone, tablet, or laptop anytime.

All courses are 100% self-paced high-definition video modules that you can watch whenever you have 10 quiet minutes. You also receive lifetime access and free updates whenever we revise the curriculum.

Yes, absolutely! We strongly encourage both parents to watch the modules together so everyone is on the same page with routines, communication, and discipline.
