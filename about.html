Why We Exist

A haven for honest parenting realities.

Real life. Real feelings. Real connection.

When you become a parent, the world showers you with unsolicited advice, rigid milestones, and picture-perfect imagery. Yet in the quiet hours of the night—when facing post-partum anxiety, an unexpected diagnosis, adoption paperwork, or simply wondering if you did enough today—what parents crave most is not perfection, but camaraderie.

WeTheMums is an editorial storytelling platform devoted to the whole spectrum of family life: maternal transitions, modern fatherhood, fertility journeys, grief, neurodiversity, and the tender humour of everyday routine.

Guiding Compass

Our Core Editorial Values

These non-negotiable principles guide every article we publish and every story we receive, creating a kinder space for every family.

01

Radical Empathy

We provide a zero-judgment environment. No shaming, no moral superiority, and no reductive comparisons.

02

Contributor Dignity

Stories are vulnerable gifts. Contributors retain control over attribution, pen names, and preview approval.

03

Inclusive Family Models

We honor biological, adoptive, blended, single, and foster families across diverse Indian and global experiences.

04

Clear Medical Separation

Personal experiences are never presented as clinical medical advice. We encourage readers to consult qualified healthcare professionals.

05

Honest Commercial Integrity

Brand collaborations and sponsored content are disclosed transparently. Commercial interests never dictate our editorial truth.

06

Privacy by Design

Personal contact details and private editorial discussions are handled carefully and protected with appropriate access controls.

Every family deserves to be heard, understood, and respected.
