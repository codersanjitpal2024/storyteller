Our Story & Purpose

Every parenting journey deserves to be heard.

We founded WeTheMums on a simple, enduring conviction: parenthood is too raw, complex, and profound to be reduced to filtered highlight reels.

Why We Exist

A haven for honest parenting realities.

When you become a parent, the world showers you with unsolicited advice, rigid milestones, and picture-perfect imagery. Yet in the quiet hours of the night—when facing post-partum anxiety, an unexpected diagnosis, adoption paperwork, or simply wondering if you did enough today—what parents crave most is not perfection, but camaraderie.

WeTheMums is an editorial media platform devoted to the whole spectrum of family life: maternal transitions, modern fatherhood, fertility journeys, grief, neurodiversity, and the tender humor of everyday routine.

Guiding Compass

Our Core Editorial Values

These non-negotiable principles guide every article we publish and every story we receive.

1. Radical Empathy

We provide a zero-judgment environment. No shaming, no moral superiority, and no reductive comparisons.

2. Contributor Dignity

Stories are vulnerable gifts. Contributors retain complete say over attribution, pen names, and preview approval.

3. Inclusive Family Models

We honor biological, adoptive, blended, single, and foster families across the multifaceted Indian and global experience.

4. Clear Medical Separation

First-person lived stories are never confused with clinical medical advice. We urge readers to consult certified healthcare specialists.

5. Honest Commercial Integrity

Any brand collaboration or sponsored series is disclosed openly. We never allow commercial interests to compromise editorial truth.

6. Privacy by Design

Personal emails, contact details, and internal editorial deliberations are protected with strict access controls.
