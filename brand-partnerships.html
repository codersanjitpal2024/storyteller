Frequently Asked Questions

How does WeTheMums ensure campaign authenticity?

We co-create stories with real parents or vetted editorial writers. We never post generic scripts or forced testimonials; every narrative centers on relatable family experiences.

What is the standard turnaround time?

Editorial features typically require 2 to 3 weeks from brief alignment to final publishing, allowing time for editorial writing, review, and client feedback.

Can we request a media kit?

Yes! Simply indicate in the enquiry form above or email us at editorial@wethemums.com, and our partnerships team will share our current campaign options.
